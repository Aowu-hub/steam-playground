📖 How to play
  • Move: W A S D (or the arrows when you play alone). On a tablet, drag on your side of the court.
  • Hit: walk into the ball — your avatar swings by itself. Moving forward into the ball = topspin (red trail), back = backspin (blue), sideways = curve (green).
  • Shots: 💥 Smash (F) when the ball is high · 🪶 Drop (G) short and soft · 🌈 Lob (T) high over them · 🔪 Slice (H) low backspin. Press just before you hit. On Hard and up, a shot pressed right at the hit = PERFECT!
  • Power orbs: at 6 / 10 / 15 hits in a rally an orb floats over the court — hit the ball through it to win a power, then press E (or ⚡).
  • Watch the shadow — it shows where the ball is. The higher the ball, the further the ball is from its shadow.
