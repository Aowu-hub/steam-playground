📖 How to play
  • Make words: put letters in one line (across or down) so they make a real word, like CAT. 🔊 The word is read out when you play it.
  • Drag: slide a letter along your rack to put your hand in order, drag it onto a square to place it, drag a yellow letter back to the rack (or to another square), or drop letters in the 🔁 swap box to change them.
  • Your player: waits on the bench doing silly moves — when you play, they run along your word counting the points! Big words (25+ or all 7 letters) start the picture set's big show (🌳 garden tree, 🐉 castle dragon, 🌊 ocean wave…). Tap ⏩ to skip.
  • Place letters: tap a letter in your rack, then tap a square. Or tap a square first (tap again to switch → / ↓) and then tap letters — they go in one after another. Tap a yellow letter to take it back. Keyboard: type the letters, Enter plays.
  • First word goes over the ⭐ in the middle. After that every new word must use or touch a letter already on the board, like a crossword — and every word it makes must be real.
  • Points: each letter shows its points (Q and Z are worth 10!). Bonus squares say what they do: ×2 LETTER / ×3 LETTER = that one letter scores 2 or 3 times its points · ×2 WORD / ×3 WORD = the whole word scores 2 or 3 times (⭐ in the middle = word ×2). Bonuses only count on the turn you cover them. Use all 7 letters in one go: +40 bonus!
  • ✅ Check tells you if your word is in our word list before you play (green = yes, red = not yet). 💡 Hint shows a word you could make. 🔁 Swap changes letters (uses your turn).
  • Win: have more points than the robot after the last round. Stars: ⭐⭐⭐ = win by a lot.
  • Levels: the robot knows more and longer words each level · Hard: big 15 × 15 board · Extreme adds 2 ★ blank tiles (any letter you like) · 💀 Hardcore: no Check, no Hint, the best robot — one wrong word ends the game. Points ×3!
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Play together: 2–4 players take turns on one device — pass it on when it's the next player's turn.
