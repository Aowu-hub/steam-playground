📖 How to play
  • Flip: tap a card (or use the arrow keys + Enter/Space). Then flip another one.
  • Match: two the same? They stay up and you go again. Not the same? They flip back — remember where they were!
  • Streaks: matches in a row give bonus points. Fewer mistakes = more stars.
  • Friends: up to 4 players take turns. Most pairs wins!
  • Hard + Extreme: after a miss, two hidden cards swap places — watch the glow!
