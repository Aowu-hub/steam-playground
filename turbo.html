📖 How to play
  • Drive: ⬅️ ➡️ steer, ⬆️ go faster, ⬇️ slow down (W A S D work too — change them with ⌨️ Keys). On a phone use the big buttons.
  • Clock: reach each 🏁 checkpoint to get more time. Get to the finish before it runs out!
  • Jump (Space / 🦘) hops over cones, rocks and even cars. Ramps send you flying higher.
  • Drift (Shift / 🌀) + steer for sharp turns and bonus points.
  • Watch out: oil makes you spin, water slows you, ⚡ boost pads make you zoom.
  • Harder races: rival racers, tunnels, floods, thunderstorms and falling rocks — look for the red warning circle!
  • Busy road: 🚨 someone driving AT you (change lane — a close dodge = bonus!), 🚑 a siren behind you (move aside = "Good driver!"), trucks dropping things, 🚧 road works (lane closed → follow the arrows, 🚩 slow zone = bonus if you go under the sign).
  • Weather (Intermediate+): 💨 wind, 🌧️ rain, ☄️ comets, 🌫️ fog, ❄️ ice, 🌪️ tornado or ⚡ storm — one per race.
  • 🕳️ Secret passages: look for a glowing clue at the side of the road and drive onto it — no traffic inside, coins and +300! 9 to find in every world.
  • 👥 2 players: in the garage tap 👥 — one keyboard, split screen, first to the 🏁 wins.
