📖 How to play
  • 3 touches a side: 🚀 Drive serves it over · 🤚 the 1st touch bumps it up · 🪶 the 2nd sets it high · 💥 the 3rd dunks it over the net!
  • Keys: W A S D move · J 🤚 touch (bump / set / dig — picked by the ball's height) · K 💥 drive / dunk (hold K on a serve = more power) · L 🪶 dink (a soft trick shot) · Space ⬆️ jump / block · Shift 🤸 dive · F 🔥 Pyra.
  • 🔥 Pyra meter: every good touch fills your flame. Half full → tap 🔥, and your next move becomes a Pyra… move (Pyra…Dunk, Pyra…Dig…) — faster, safer, harder to stop.
  • Point: the ball lands in their court = your point. Out, in the net or a 4th touch = their point.
  • 🌱 Basic: the ball waits for you — the button to press glows. Hints fade from Junior.
