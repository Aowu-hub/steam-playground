Sweet Swap

Swap two pictures to make a row of 3 — then watch them pop!

👤
1. Pick your pictures
2. Pick a level
📖 How to play
  • Swap: tap a picture, then tap the one next to it (or slide your finger from one to the other). If it makes a row of 3 or a 2 × 2 square of the same, they pop! If not, they swap back and the move is free.
  • Goals: each level shows what to collect at the top (like 🍓 × 12) or blockers to break — 🧊 ice box, 📦 package, ⛓️ metal cage, 🔥 flame cage, 🔒 space prison, ⛽ gas out or ☄️ meteor cage (pop the picture underneath). Finish the goals before the moves run out.
  • Super pictures: 2 × 2 square = 🏀 bouncer (bounces 4 times and pops a + shape each time, aiming for blockers) · 4 in a row = ✚ cross zapper (pops its whole row AND column) · L or T shape = 💥 bomb (3 × 3; two bombs swapped together = 5 × 5!) · 5 in a row = 🌈 rainbow (swap it with a picture to pop every one of those) · 6 or more at once = ✨ power cell with its own effect in every picture set: 🍯 Nectar rain · ⭐ Spinning star · 🦮 Super collar · 🧬 DNA upgrade (makes new super pictures!) · 🦠 Virus · ⚡ Power node · 🛡️ EMP shield. Swap two super pictures together for a giant blast.
  • 2 stages: Extreme and 💀 Hardcore have two stages — finish stage 1 and new blockers drop on the board with new goals (Extreme gives bonus moves).
  • Sweet finish: moves you have left at the end turn into bonus points. Stars: ⭐⭐⭐ = lots of moves left.
  • 💡 Hint: wiggles a swap you can make (on Basic–Hard it shows by itself if you wait). No swaps left? The board mixes itself.
  • 💀 Hardcore: opens after Extreme — big board, double blockers, 2 stages, no hints, no bonus moves. Points ×3!
  • Keyboard: arrows move the yellow box, Space picks a picture, then an arrow swaps it.
