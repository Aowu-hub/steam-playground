📖 How TD-Stories works
  • Defend: enemies walk along the paths to your tower. Your heroes stop them — and nobody gets hurt, they just give up and fly away!
  • Skills: before every wave each hero levels up: pick ONE card. Tap the round skill buttons to use them.
  • Colours: 🔴 Attack · 🔵 Helpers · 🟤 Traps · 🟢 Shields · 🟣 Charms — one skill of each colour, up to Lv 3.
  • Evolve: at tier 5 your heroes evolve and you pick a main colour; at tier 10 they get their ULTIMATE.
