Block Drop

Turn and drop the falling blocks — fill a whole row to clear it!

👤
1. Pick your blocks
2. Pick a level
📖 How to play
  • Goal: clear the number of rows shown on the level. A row full from side to side disappears!
  • Move: on a touch screen use the game pad — the round thumb pad on the left: ◀ ▶ slide, ▼ makes the block fall (keep holding ▼ and it falls faster and faster — it turns gold); roll your thumb around it without lifting. The round buttons on the right: A ⟳ turn, B ⟲ turn the other way. Tap the HOLD box to keep a block for later, ⏸ at the top pauses. You can also swipe the board: tap = turn, swipe down = drop. Keyboard: ← → ↓, ↑ / X turn, Z turn back, Space drop, C hold.
  • Keys: ← → move · ↑/X turn · Z turn back · ↓ faster · Space drop · C hold · P pause
  • Points: 1 row 100 · 2 rows 300 · 3 rows 500 · 4 rows at once = BLOCK BLAST 800 (× level).
  • 🛟 Saves: on the first levels, if the blocks reach the top, a save clears the bottom rows so you can keep going.
  • 👻 Ghost shows where the block will land (up to Hard). 💀 Hardcore: fastest, no ghost, no hold, no saves — points ×3!
