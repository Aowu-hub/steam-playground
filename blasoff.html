📖 How to play
  • Run: W A S D (or the arrows when you play alone) · Shift = sprint. On a tablet: the 🕹️ stick — push it all the way to sprint.
  • With the ball: J pass (hold = longer) · K shoot (hold = harder) · L trick · J + L trick pass · K + L trick shot.
  • Space jump · Space, Space flick the ball up → then K = volley shot · J = volley pass.
  • Without the ball: J tackle · K slide tackle (Intermediate and up) · L call a teammate to help · C switch player · Space jump over a slide or head a high ball.
  • 📣 Shout to your keeper: when they shoot at your goal, press Q or E for the side the ball is going — your robot keeper dives that way!
