📖 How to play
  1. Pieces move one square diagonally forward on the dark squares.
  2. Jump over a piece next to you to capture it. If you can jump, you must jump (it glows orange).
  3. After a jump, keep jumping if you can — that is a double jump!
  4. Reach the far side and you become a King 👑. Kings can move backwards too.
  5. Win by taking every piece, or by leaving the other side with no moves.
