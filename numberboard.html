📖 How to play
  • Make a number sentence: put tiles in one line (across or down) so they read like 3 + 4 = 7. Every sentence needs an = and both sides must make the same number.
  • Drag: slide a tile along your rack to put your hand in order (like 3 + 4 = 7), drag it onto a square to place it, drag a yellow tile back to the rack (or to another square), or drop tiles in the 🔁 swap box to change them.
  • Your player: waits on the bench doing silly moves — when you play, they run along your number sentence counting the points! Big plays (25+ or all 8 tiles) start the board's big show (🌳 oak tree, 🐒 jungle swing, 🌊 ocean wave, 🤖 robot build…). Tap ⏩ to skip.
  • Place tiles: tap a tile in your rack, then tap a square. Or tap a square first (tap again to switch → / ↓) and then tap tiles — they go in one after another. Tap a yellow tile to take it back. Keyboard: type numbers and signs, Enter plays.
  • First move goes over the ⭐ in the middle. After that every new sentence must use a tile already on the board, like a crossword.
  • Big numbers: number tiles next to each other join up: 1 then 2 = 12. Number tiles 10–20 stand on their own.
  • Points: each tile shows its points. Bonus squares say what they do: ×2 TILE / ×3 TILE = that one tile scores 2 or 3 times its points · ×2 ALL / ×3 ALL = the whole number sentence scores 2 or 3 times (⭐ in the middle = all ×2). Bonuses only count on the turn you cover them. Use all 8 tiles in one go: +40 bonus!
  • ✅ Check tells you if your sentence is right before you play (green = right, red = not yet). 💡 Hint shows a move you could make. 🔁 Swap changes tiles (uses your turn).
  • Win: have more points than the robot after the last round. Stars: ⭐⭐⭐ = win by a lot.
  • Levels: Basic + and = with 0–9 · Junior adds − and 10 · Intermediate adds × and 11–12 · Hard: big 15 × 15 board, ÷ and tiles up to 20 · Extreme adds 2 ★ blank tiles (any tile you like) · 💀 Hardcore: no Check, no Hint, the best robot — one wrong sentence ends the game. Points ×3!
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Play together: 2–4 players take turns on one device — pass it on when it's the next player's turn.
