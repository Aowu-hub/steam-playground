📖 How to play
  • Balls: Basic, Junior and Intermediate give you 3 balls, Hard 2, Extreme just 1. 🛟 Ball Saver catches a ball that falls down the middle: Basic has one for every ball, Junior has one for the whole game, harder levels have none.
  • Flippers: tap the left or right half of the screen, or press ← → (or Z and /).
  • Launch: hold the right side (or Space), then let go — longer hold, stronger launch. Hit the blinking top lane for a Skill Shot!
  • Lucky Lights: hit the glowing spot, then the next one… 3 in a row = ×2 points, 5 = Multiball, 10 = JACKPOT 🎰.
  • Secret Hole: hit all 3 targets to open it, then sink the ball for a 20-second bonus scene.
  • Combos: keep hitting things quickly — 5 hits ×2, 10 ×3, 20 ×5, 35 ×10!
  • Spell the word in the top lanes, open 🎁 Mystery Boxes, fill your Buddy for a ×2 bonus, and use Nudge (3 per ball).
