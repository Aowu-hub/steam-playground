📖 How the lab works
  • 1 · Pick a test + a thing: any thing you made in 🏭 Artifactory — or a 🧪 lab sample if you have nothing yet.
  • 2 · Set it in the rig: tap 🗜️ when it is in the green middle.
  • 3 · Guess first: 👍 / 👎 — scientists always guess before they test (that's a hypothesis). A right guess = bonus points!
  • 4 · Watch the gauge and read your 📋 test card: the number, a ⭐ family record and why it happened.
  • 🧪 = can break. A broken thing is never lost — fix it with 🔧 Repair in Artifactory.
  • 🌱 Basic: the next step glows and your lab buddy gives hints. From ⚡ Hard you guess a range instead of yes / no.
