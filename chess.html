📖 How to play
  1. Every piece moves its own way — tap a piece and glowing dots show where it can go (and a tip explains it).
  2. Move onto an enemy piece to take it.
  3. Check = a King is under attack. If your King is in check you must save it!
  4. Checkmate = the King is in check and can't escape. That wins the game.
  5. A Pawn that reaches the far side becomes a Queen (or another piece). On the big board the King can castle — jump 2 squares toward a Rook to hide.
  6. Basic is mini chess: a 6×6 board with no Bishops — perfect to learn.
