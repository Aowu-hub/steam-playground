Pose Party

Pose Party uses the camera, so it opens on its own camera page.

👋 Who's playing?

👥 Tap up to 4 for a party — you take turns on one camera.

⏳ Finding the players…
  • 📷 Tap the button, then Allow the camera.
  • 🧍 Stand back so the camera sees you.
  • 🏠 When you're done, tap Gameroom to come back.

The camera picture stays on this device.
