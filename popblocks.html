📖 How to play
  • Pop: tap 2 or more touching blocks that are the same (💀 Hardcore: 3 or more). Blocks above fall down, empty columns slide together.
  • Big groups = big points: 2 blocks = 20, 5 = 125, 10 = 500! Pop small groups first so big ones join up.
  • Power blocks: pop 5–6 → 🚀 rocket (clears a row or column) · 7–9 → 💣 bomb (clears around it) · 10+ → 🌈 rainbow (pops every block of one kind). Tap a power block to fire it. Two power blocks touching = a super blast!
  • Missions: the ticks at the top — pop some of one kind, break 🧊 ice (pop next to it), smash 📦 crates, open 🔒 locks, bring 🐣 chicks down to the bottom.
  • Win: reach the 🎯 target and finish every mission. When no groups are left, the round ends. Clear the whole board for a huge bonus! 🎉
  • 🔥 Combo: pop again quickly for ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5 points.
  • ↶ Undo: on Basic and Junior. 💀 Hardcore: 6 kinds, groups of 3+, a move limit, no undo — points ×3!
