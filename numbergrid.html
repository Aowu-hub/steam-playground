Fill the grid — every row, column and box gets each one once!

👤
1. Pick your pictures
2. 🗺️ Pick a map (bigger map = ×more points)
3. 🎚️ Pick a difficulty
📖 How to play
  • The rule: every row ↔, every column ↕ and every thick-lined box has each picture exactly once.
  • Play: tap an empty square, then tap a picture at the bottom. Or tap a picture first, then tap squares to paint it in. Keys: arrows + 1–9, Backspace to erase.
  • 🗺️ Map, then 🎚️ difficulty: pick the size (4 × 4 · 5 × 5 · 6 × 6 · 9 × 9 — a bigger map gives ×1.25 · ×1.5 · ×2 points), then how hard: more or fewer clues, how mistakes show, notes and hints.
  • Wrong? Basic to Intermediate: it bounces back — try again. Hard and Extreme: it turns red until you fix it. Every miss costs a star.
  • ✏️ Notes (from Intermediate): write small guesses in a square.
  • 💡 Hint: fills one square for you (max 2 stars).
  • Drag: drag a picture from the bar onto a square to drop it in — every picture set lands its own way (🐾 hop, 💥 splat, 💨 skid, ✨ poof, 🌠 beam…). Drag a picture you placed to move it, or off the board to take it back.
  • A full row, column or box does a happy wave — and your player jumps off the bench and runs along it! 🎉
  • 💀 Hardcore: opens after Extreme. The fewest clues (17 on 9 × 9 — the fewest possible!), no notes check, no hints, nothing is marked. Fill it all, then press ✅ Submit — one wrong square and it's over. Points ×3!
