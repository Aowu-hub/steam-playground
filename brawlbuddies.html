👤
Pick a level — 🤖 a random family member in a robot helmet; the helmet evolves and gets tougher
📖 How to play
  • Bonk your buddy until their ❤️ bar is empty — Bonked out! No real hurting, just cartoon stars. ⭐
  • Block by walking back (away from your buddy). Duck + back = sitting block for low bonks. Jump attacks must be blocked standing.
  • Every hit fills your ⚡ bar. Full bar = Power Push!
  • 🚀 Uppercut sends your buddy flying — hit them in the air to keep them bouncing, up to 5 bounces (①…⑤) = 🌟 Sky Combo!
  • 💨 Dodge-roll: a quick roll you can't be hit in. Hold forward to roll behind your buddy. Wait a moment before the next roll.
