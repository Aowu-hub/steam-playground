📖 How to play
  • Hit the beat: beats fly out from the middle to the 4 corner buttons. Tap the button (or press its key) just as the beat reaches it — the ring around the button shows when. Perfect · Great · Good · Miss; hits in a row build your 🔥 combo.
  • Keys: Z top-left · X bottom-left · / top-right · . bottom-right · C and , the pop-up buttons · Space the 🔴 big beat · Left / Right Shift for slides. Change them in 🎹 Keys.
  • 💃 Dance pad: press Space (or tap the middle) any time — your player jumps into the middle and does an epic dance (on Intermediate and up the middle is also the 🔴 big beat).
  • Hold beats (Junior +): keep holding until the tail is gone. Doubles (Intermediate +): two buttons at once. 🔴 Big beat (Intermediate +): a big ring closes in on the middle — hit Space (or tap the middle).
  • Slides (Hard +): a button rides a curvy line — drag it with your finger, or hold Left Shift (left buttons) / Right Shift (right buttons) until it is back. ⬆ +2: two pop-up buttons rise on the sides for a while.
  • Extreme: the buttons ride into the middle as a 2 × 2 and beats fly in from the edges, then they ride back out — and sometimes the ring spins so the beats make a spiral. 💀 Hardcore: faster, no rings, 3 misses in a row ends the song, ×3 points.
  • Looks: 🕺 Boy vs Girl dance-off · 🍉 Fruit vs Sweets · 🚀 Spacecraft vs Asteroid · 🦖 Dinosaur × Jungle · 🤖 Robot × Apocalypse · 🐠 Under the sea vs Beach — the left buttons are one side, the right buttons the other. The music stays the same.
  • 🎹 Piano: notes fall onto the keys — play each one when it touches the keyboard. Keys: A S D F G H J K L ; ' are the white keys (C D E F G A B C D E F), W E T Y U O P the black keys. Left / Right Shift = octave down / up (Extreme +), Space = ✨ big-line note (tap, or hold for a long one). On a phone: play the drawn piano, swipe the strip (or ◀ ▶) to move along it, tap the falling area for ✨. 6 songs per level, pass 3 to clear it. 🎵 Free play: ⏺ record your own tune and ▶ play it back.
  • 🥁 Drums: a drum kit seen from above that grows every level (3 → 12 pieces). A dot grows from the middle of a drum — hit it when the dot fills the drum (the rim glows = Perfect). Keys: Q crash · W small tom · E medium tom · R ride · T crash 2 · A hi-hat · S snare · D floor tom · F splash · Z hi-hat pedal · Space kick · X 2nd kick (the letters fade after 3 games — ❓ brings them back). On a phone: tap the drums, the kick + pedals are wide foot bars at the bottom. Beats: 🎸 rock · 🎤 pop · 🥁 march · 💃 samba · 🎷 swing · 🪘 klong-yao · 🤖 electro, plus drumming along to the piano songs (🎹).
  • 🎼 Song Maker: 🎹 Piano → ⏺ Make a song: pick a 🥁 speed, wait for the 4 clicks, play your tune, tap ⏹ — the notes snap to the beat. Or ✏️ Write a song / ✏️ edit one of yours on the note grid: tap a square to add a note, tap a note to take it away, drag it to move it, drag its right end to make it longer, ▶ to hear it (🔁 loops the part you can see). Give it a name + a picture + a drum beat and 💾 save it. Everyone sees it on the 🎼 Family songs shelf (Piano, Drums and Band) and plays it at their own level (easier levels leave out the hardest notes). ❤️ like it, 🏆 top 3, ⬇ download it as a .mid file (GarageBand, MuseScore …) or 📥 import a .mid.
  • 🎸 Band (2 players): one song, two parts — 🎹 piano on the letter keys + 🥁 drums on the number pad (a big keyboard's, or a plug-in USB number pad: 7 crash · 8 / 9 toms · − ride · 4 hi-hat · 5 snare · 6 floor tom · + crash 2 · 1 pedal · 0 kick · . 2nd kick · 3 splash), or the drummer taps the kit on a touch screen. Band score + each player's own score; both players on stage. Band songs open with the 🎹 Piano levels.
  • Songs: 10 songs per level, ★1 to ★10 (more stars = harder). Pass a song to open the next; pass 5 to clear the level and open the next one.
