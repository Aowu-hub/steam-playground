📖 How to play
  • Two pictures look the same — but a few things changed. Poke each change, in either picture.
  • 🌱 Basic: 🎨 colours + ➖➕ missing / extra things · 🐣 Junior adds 📏 size + 🔺 shape · 🔦 Intermediate adds 🔄 turn / flip + 🔢 count.
  • 📸 Family trip scene: YOUR family's avatars pose for a photo at 7 trip places (🐘 jungle temple, 🌸 cherry mountain, 🐪 pyramids, ❄️ northern lights, 🌋 lava isles, 🌊 coral coast, 🚀 moon base) — find the 🕺 new pose, 😉 new face, 🔄 turned person, and the changed suitcases, cameras and ice creams.
  • 🔍 Magnifier: stuck? It shows you one change (a few per picture).
  • 👁️ Blink: ONE picture flips between A and B — poke what changes. Harder levels put a blank blink in between, so the change hides!
  • ⏱️ Photo Hunt Arcade: a speed run — race through 10 to 15 pictures to the 🏁 finish. The clock counts up, every 5 pictures is a ✅ checkpoint split, from ⚡ Hard a wrong poke adds seconds, and on 🌋 / 💀 every find gives ⏳ +3 s more time left. Beat your best time for ⭐⭐⭐!
  • 🎯 Odd One Out: all the cards are alike but one — poke it. From ⚡ Hard: 🧮 the sum that isn't right, 🔤 the word that doesn't rhyme · 🌋 Extreme: 🌀 the one that breaks the pattern.
  • 🕵️ Detective Adventure: a case book in 3 chapters — 🔍 find the thief's footprints, 📜 read and answer, 🚶 walk the map, 🖼️ check the camera, 🔐 crack the code and 👉 catch the thief who matches BOTH clues. Every chapter gives you a 🎓 lesson card!
  • ⚡ Hard adds 🧮 sums and 🔤 words on little signs · 🌋 Extreme adds 🌀 bead patterns.
  • A wrong poke is just an "oops" — but fewer oops = more ⭐. 💀 Hardcore: no magnifier, 3 oops and the case is lost.
  • Every picture is new — made up on the spot, never the same twice!
