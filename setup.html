📲 Offline app

The games also install as an app (iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen) that plays without internet and syncs to this Sheet: aowu-hub.github.io/steam-playground. A grown-up types the family key once on each device — it links that device to the family's players and results. Without it the app is a 🧪 guest copy (games play, nothing syncs).
