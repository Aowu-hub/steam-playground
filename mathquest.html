📖 How to play
  • Answer: tap the right number (or press 1, 2, 3, 4). Tap 🔊 to hear the question again.
  • Pictures: on the first levels you can count the pictures, hop along the number line, count the rows or share into baskets.
  • Points: ➕ ×1 · ➖ ×1.15 · ✖️ ×1.5 · ➗ ×1.6 · 🔀 Mixed ×1.8 — harder maths scores more.
  • Streaks: right answers in a row give bonus points 🔥. Finish before the clock runs out for a time bonus.
  • Stars: more right answers and more time left = more stars. Get most of them right to pass the level.
  • 💡 Hint: takes away one wrong answer (3 per round).
  • ✖️ ➗ Tables: Hard uses the 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 13 times tables (no easy 10s), Extreme 9 and 12–17, Hardcore 12–19.
  • ⏱️ Minute Challenge: opens after Intermediate. 60 seconds of mixed sums (tables 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12) — a mistake doesn't end it, but costs 50 points. Count your ✅ and beat your best!
  • 💀 Hardcore: opens after Extreme. The biggest numbers, the fastest clock, no hints — one wrong answer ends the round. Points ×3!
