📖 How to play
  • Ways to play: 🔢 Numbers (🧮 Classic sums, ⏱️ Minute Challenge) · 🔤 English (Word Sums — the sum is written in words) · 🐘 ไทย (เลขคำไทย — the sum in Thai words, Thai digits ๐–๙ from 🌋 Extreme) · 📖 Story Problems / 📜 โจทย์ปัญหา (real-life stories) · 🤔 Trick Questions / 🙃 คำถามหลอก (think twice!) · 📝 Test Practice / 📝 ข้อสอบ (school-style tests). 🔜 = coming soon.
  • Answer: 👆 tap the right answer (or press 1, 2, 3, 4) — or ⌨️ type it on the number pad (Enter = ✓). ✨ Auto = tap on the first levels, type from ⚡ Hard; 💀 Hardcore is always typed. Typed answers score ×1.25. Tap 🔊 to hear the question again.
  • Word Sums: 🌱 🐣 find the number · 🔦 sometimes the sum is in numbers and you pick the WORD · ⚡ and up you pick the answer word — watch out for spelling traps (fourty, สิบหนึ่ง)! Word Sums score ×1.1.
  • Story Problems · โจทย์ปัญหา: read the story, work out the answer. 🌱 🐣 🔦 one step (🔦 the words don't always say "more" or "left") · ⚡ two steps with 🪙 baht and ⏰ minutes · 🌋 💀 up to three steps and 🙈 one number that doesn't matter! After every answer the 🪜 steps line shows the way. More time on the clock, ×1.3 points.
  • Test Practice · ข้อสอบ: questions like a real school maths test — 📝 Test Practice in the style of Western (US) schools, 📝 ข้อสอบ in the style of Thai schools. 🌱 Grade 1–2 / ป.1–2 · 🐣 Grade 3–4 / ป.3–4 · 🔦 Grade 5–6 / ป.5–6 · ⚡ Grade 7–8 / ม.1–2 (more grades coming up to Grade 12 / ม.6, and 💀 real-life scenarios). From 🔦 the ⌨️ pad has . and − keys for decimals and negative numbers. Clocks, graphs, shapes and money are drawn on the card; after every answer the 🪜 steps show how. ×1.4 points.
  • Trick Questions · คำถามหลอก: the quick answer is a trap — read it again! Tap your answer: 🕵️ spot the trick and the crowd cheers; fall for it and you see the 🤔 trap and ✅ why. A trick doesn't come back until you've seen all the tricks of that level. More time on the clock, ×1.3 points.
  • Every round is new: questions from your last 3 rounds of that level don't come back.
  • Pictures: on the first levels you can count the pictures, hop along the number line, count the rows or share into baskets.
  • Points: ➕ ×1 · ➖ ×1.15 · ✖️ ×1.5 · ➗ ×1.6 · 🔀 Mixed ×1.8 — harder maths scores more.
  • Streaks: right answers in a row give bonus points 🔥. Finish before the clock runs out for a time bonus.
  • Stars: more right answers and more time left = more stars. Get most of them right to pass the level.
  • 💡 Hint: takes away one wrong answer (3 per round).
  • ✖️ ➗ Tables: Hard uses the 7, 8, 9, 11, 12 and 13 times tables (no easy 10s), Extreme 9 and 12–17, Hardcore 12–19.
  • ⏱️ Minute Challenge: opens after Intermediate. 60 seconds of mixed sums (tables 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12) — a mistake doesn't end it, but costs 50 points. Count your ✅ and beat your best!
  • 💀 Hardcore: opens after Extreme. The biggest numbers, the fastest clock, no hints — one wrong answer ends the round. Points ×3!
