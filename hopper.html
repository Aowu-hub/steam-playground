📖 How to play
  • Hop: arrow keys or W A S D (change them with ⌨️ Keys). On a phone: swipe, tap to hop forward, or use the arrow buttons.
  • Cross: dodge the traffic, ride the logs and pads across water (don't fall in!), and watch for the flashing lights before a fast one zooms by.
  • Checkpoints: every 25 rows 🏁 — bonus points, and you come back there if you get bonked.
  • Goal: reach the row goal to win the level, then keep going for a high score. 🪙 coins and 💎 gems give extra points.
  • Have fun: X (or 🎲) does one of 30 moves. Standing still for a bit, your hopper does a move by itself.
  • Harder levels: the screen starts moving (don't fall behind!), sneaky traps 🍌 🕳️ and falling surprises — watch for the red warning circle.
