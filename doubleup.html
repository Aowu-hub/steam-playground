📖 How to play
  • Slide: swipe ↑ ↓ ← → (or use the arrow buttons / arrow keys). Every tile slides as far as it can.
  • Double up: two tiles that are the same join into one bigger tile (2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8 …). In picture themes they grow up: 🥚 → 🐣 → 🐥 → 🐔 …
  • Win: make the goal tile shown on the level. After every slide a new small tile appears — when the board is full and nothing can join, the game is over.
  • ↶ Undo: as many as you like on Basic and Junior, 3 on Intermediate, 1 on Hard. Stars: ⭐⭐⭐ = no undo used.
  • 🪨 Rock (Extreme): a rock that never moves — slide around it!
  • 💀 Hardcore: make 4096, no undo — points ×3. 🏔️ Challenge: a tiny 3 × 3 board — can you reach 1024? (2048 can't fit on 3 × 3!)
  • Tip: keep your biggest tile in a corner and build towards it.
